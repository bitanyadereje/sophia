# Sophia

An Orthodox reading platform.

Browse a catalog of Orthodox books. Write reviews, quote passages, cite
sources. Discuss what you're reading — every book has a thread, and
there's room for general conversation outside the books too. Follow
curated reading paths that move through a topic in order, from the
Fathers to modern writers.

Track what you're reading on a shelf, keep private notes as you go, and
set a reading goal for the year.

Built for readers who want somewhere quieter than a general book site —
somewhere the tradition is taken seriously and the discussion is serious
without being cold.

## What it does

**Books.** A catalog you can browse and search. Each book has a page
with its author, description, cover, page count, language, and the tags
that place it — patristic, ascetic, liturgical, whatever fits. Readers
add books as they find them; the community and moderators keep the
catalog clean over time.jyt

**Reviews.** Write a review of anything you've read. Give it a rating,
pull out a passage you want to remember, cite where it came from. Other
readers can like reviews, comment on them, and save them for later.
You can edit or delete your own. One review per person per book, so the
ratings stay honest.

**Discussions.** Every book has a single thread — created automatically
when the book is added — where all conversation about it lives. Replies
nest. Posts can be liked and saved. Alongside the per-book threads,
there's a general space for questions that don't belong to any one book:
"What does the Church mean by theosis?" "Where should I start with the
Philokalia?"

**Reading paths.** Curated sequences of books on a topic, arranged in
order, with a note explaining why each book is where it is. Paths are
written by curators and reviewed before they go public, so browsing them
isn't a gamble. Each book inside a path links back to its discussion
thread, so you can read and talk at the same time.

**Your library.** Every book you add lands on a shelf: want to read,
reading, finished, or did not finish. Track your page progress. Keep
private notes on any book — reflections, quotes, questions — visible
only to you. Set a reading goal for the year and watch it fill up.

**Search.** Search across books, reviews, discussion posts, and reading
paths. Filter by tags to narrow things down. Everything is one query
away.

**Bilingual.** The whole interface works in English and Amharic.

**Themes.** Eight color themes, from light to navy to vintage, so you
can read in whatever feels right.

**AI, later.** Ask a question and get an answer drawn only from books in
the catalog, with citations. Get recommendations for a topic or a
reading level. This is stubbed but not finished — the goal is a research
assistant that stays inside the tradition rather than wandering off.

## Who it's for

Orthodox readers who want a place to keep track of what they've read,
write about it seriously, and talk about it with other people who are
reading the same things. Not a general book site with an Orthodox
section bolted on — an Orthodox site from the ground up.
